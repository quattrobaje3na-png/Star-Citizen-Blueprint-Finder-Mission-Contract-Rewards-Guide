⚠️ Contract Reward Advisory Completing a contract grants you a random blueprint from the reward pool tied to that specific mission. If you do not receive the specific blueprint you are looking for, you will need to repeat the contract until it drops. The good news is, most of the pools consist of full armor sets and a handful of weapons. If you do not see the exact contract you need repeat existing contracts for that faction until it appears.

🔧 Crafting Bench Every blueprint in the build, with the materials it consumes and the stats it is predicted to produce. Deviations sum within a stat and different stats multiply, so DPS is damage multiplied by fire rate rather than the two added together. Quality is modelled per slot, because each slot consumes a different material and the slots do not move the same stats: on the P4-AR the frame and stock shift recoil, and only the barrel touches damage. Drag a bar or type an exact number. Quality 500 is the neutral point: the curves are authored to sit at 1.0 there, so a blueprint reading 0% across the board at 500 is correct rather than broken.
