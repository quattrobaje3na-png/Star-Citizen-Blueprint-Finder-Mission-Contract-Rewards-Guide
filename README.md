The Ultimate 4.X Mission Intel Tool for Star Citizen Crafting.
Updated for 4.10.

🛠️ CONTRACT REWARDS DATABASE
Stop guessing which missions are worth your time. Choose between Weapons, Armor Sets, or Ammo & Consumables.
The tool instantly filters the database to show you exactly which mission contracts and factions provide the blueprints you are looking for.
Complete Armor Sets are intelligently weighted to the top of the list to help you finish your loadouts faster.
The tool is designed for simplicity and ease of navigation, making it useful for long time players and new players alike.

⚒️ CRAFTING BENCH
Switch modes and the same database becomes a workbench. Every one of the 1,606 craftable blueprints in the build, with the exact materials each one consumes, how long it takes, and what it is predicted to produce.

Material quality drives the result, so the bench lets you set it: one bar for everything, or a different quality per material slot. Quality 500 is the neutral point the game itself uses, so a fresh card opens showing the item exactly as it comes out of a shop, and every number after that is a change you caused. 1,323 blueprints show real before-and-after stats rather than a bare percentage.

The bench also knows which way is better. Recoil, handling, kick, spread and quantum fuel use improve as they go down, and they are coloured accordingly instead of being read as losses.

Other tools: a disassemble panel for what dismantling returns, grouped finishes so forty colorways of one item collapse to a single card, a material filter, favorites, and Show All / Unlocked Only.

🛒 CRAFTING CART & MINING ROUTES
Add anything you plan to build and the cart totals the raw materials across the whole list, then names the best places to mine each one. 46 mining locations across Stanton, Pyro and Nyx, with the yield rate and extraction method for each, sorted same-system-first. 33 of the 37 crafting materials have a known mining source; the remaining four are named rather than quietly omitted.

📦 INVENTORY & ROUTE MANAGEMENT
Never lose track of what you've already unlocked. Check off blueprints as you earn them to automatically hide them from your search results and route calculations. Your inventory saves directly to your device, and you can seamlessly import or export your progress including uploading a raw blueprints.txt file from log-scanning scripts to instantly sync your account's inventory. Pair this with the Grind Route Planner to calculate the absolute fastest mission path based on your current faction reputation.

🔄 OPTIONAL SIGN-IN
There is now a Login button, and it is entirely optional. The tool works exactly as it always has without an account: your inventory lives on your device and nothing is required of you.

Signing in adds one thing — your unlocked blueprints follow you between devices, and stay in step with the overlay if you use it. Ticking something on your desktop shows up on your phone; unticking it removes it in both places.

Blueprints are tracked by the game's own internal ID rather than by display name, so an item renamed in a patch stays where it belongs instead of quietly vanishing from your list.

📊 ABOUT THE NUMBERS
Everything here is read from the game's own shipped data for build 4.10.191.2241 — 1,606 blueprints covering 880 armour pieces, 381 ship components, 174 personal weapons, 95 ship weapons, 36 flight suits, 36 ammo types and 4 mission items.

The crafting predictions are calculated from the modifier curves the game ships, not estimated. They are corroborated against a second independent tool, but no one has yet crafted an item in game and measured the result, so treat them as a very good model rather than a certainty.
